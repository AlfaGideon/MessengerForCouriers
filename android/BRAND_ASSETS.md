# Courier-service logos

Small service marks are used only to identify the courier service selected in the local profile.

- Yandex Food, Magnit Delivery, Ozon, and Wildberries app icons: [Papirus Icons](https://github.com/PapirusDevelopmentTeam/papirus_icons), Apache-2.0; rendered locally into `app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/` PNG resources.
- Yandex Delivery mark: [Yandex Delivery logo](https://logo-teka.com/yandex-dostavka/).
- Kuper app mark: [Kuper logo](https://logo-teka.com/kuper/).
- Samokat app mark: [Samokat app listing](https://mwm.ai/apps/samokat-zakaz-i-dostavka-edy/1401500803).
- Dostavista app mark: [Dostavista courier app listing](https://andro.io/app/sebbia-delivery).

Company names and marks remain the property of their respective owners. A generic parcel symbol is used when a courier service has no bundled logo (for example, a custom service or Dostavista).
